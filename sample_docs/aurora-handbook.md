# Aurora Labs Employee Handbook

Aurora Labs is a fictional 120-person software company used as sample data for DocuChat. Nothing in this handbook describes a real organisation.

## 1. Working hours and remote work

Our core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time. Outside those hours people may organise their day as they like, provided they attend their team rituals.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams agree on at least two shared office days so that planning and design reviews happen in person. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from a department head and are reviewed every six months.

The company reimburses a one-time home office setup allowance of 25,000 rupees for a chair, desk or monitor. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.

## 2. Leave policy

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at two days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried over into the next year; anything beyond that lapses on 31 March.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is only required when a single absence lasts longer than three consecutive working days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver, both fully paid. Parental leave can be taken in up to three blocks within the first year after a birth or adoption.

Aurora Labs closes for a company-wide recharge week during the last week of December. Recharge week does not count against annual leave.

## 3. Expenses and travel

Travel must be booked through the internal travel desk at least 14 days in advance for flights. Economy class is the default for all flights under eight hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.

Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to 1,500 rupees per day in metro cities and 1,000 rupees per day elsewhere. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

Expense claims must be filed in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense with an itemised receipt. Claims older than 90 days are rejected automatically.

## 4. Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 40,000 rupees that can be used for books, online courses, certifications and conference tickets. Unused learning budget does not roll over.

Engineers may also spend every second Friday afternoon on self-directed learning, which we call Growth Fridays. Work done on Growth Fridays should be shared in the monthly demo session.

## 5. On-call and incident response

Engineering teams that own production services run a weekly on-call rotation that starts every Monday at 10:00. Each rotation has a primary and a secondary engineer.

The primary on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 15 minutes, at any time of day. If the primary does not acknowledge, the page escalates to the secondary, and after a further 15 minutes to the engineering manager.

Every on-call shift is compensated with a flat stipend of 6,000 rupees per week, plus one day of time off in lieu for every weekend incident that took more than two hours to resolve.

Incidents are classified by severity. A SEV1 is a full outage or data loss affecting customers; a SEV2 is a major feature that is degraded; a SEV3 is a minor issue with a workaround. Every SEV1 and SEV2 requires a blameless postmortem document within five working days.

## 6. Security basics

All laptops use full-disk encryption and must lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity. Hardware security keys are mandatory for access to production systems and the source code host.

Secrets such as API keys and database passwords must never be committed to a repository. They are stored in the company vault and injected into services at deploy time.

Report suspected phishing emails using the Report button in the mail client; the security team reviews every report within one business day.

## 7. Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review combines a self-assessment, feedback from two peers chosen by the employee, and an assessment by the manager.

Promotions are decided by a calibration committee after the October cycle and take effect from 1 January.
